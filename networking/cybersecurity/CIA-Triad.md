# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a fundamental concept in cybersecurity.

## 1. Confidentiality 🔒

Confidentiality means protecting information from unauthorized access.

Example:
Only authorized users should be able to access private data.

## 2. Integrity 🛡️

Integrity means keeping information accurate and protected from unauthorized changes.

Example:
A user's account information should not be changed without permission.

## 3. Availability ⚡

Availability means ensuring that systems and information are accessible when authorized users need them.

Example:
A website should remain available to legitimate users.

## Why CIA Triad is Important

The CIA Triad helps security professionals understand the three main goals of protecting information:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
